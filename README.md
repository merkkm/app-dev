# Sweet Home 🏢👹

> A Korean horror-thriller where humanity's darkest desires become literal monsters.

---

## Overview

**Sweet Home** is a South Korean horror-thriller series that premiered on Netflix in *December 2020*. It is based on the webtoon by Kim Carnby and Hwang Young-chan.

After losing his family, a reclusive teenager named **Cha Hyun-su** moves into the Green Home apartment complex. Soon, residents begin turning into grotesque monsters shaped by their deepest desires, and the survivors must band together to stay alive.

## Why I Love It

1. **The monsters** – Every creature reflects a human desire, which makes them creepy *and* meaningful.
2. **The theme** – It asks what makes someone human when the world falls apart.
3. **The tension** – Being trapped in one building makes every hallway feel dangerous.

### Main Characters

- **Cha Hyun-su** (Song Kang) – the isolated teen at the center of the story
- **Pyeon Sang-wook** (Lee Jin-wook) – a tough, mysterious ex-soldier
- **Seo Yi-kyung** (Lee Si-young) – a firefighter and one of the strongest survivors
- **Lee Eun-yu** (Go Min-si) – a young cellist trying to protect her family
